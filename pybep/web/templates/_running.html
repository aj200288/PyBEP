{# Shown while a run is going. A form POST leaves the browser sitting on the old page with nothing to show for it, sometimes for most of a minute, which reads as "nothing happened" — so the submit that starts a run puts this up and the page that answers takes it away. It sits in the corner and lets clicks through rather than covering the page. A minute is long enough that people want to go on reading the last graph, the notes on their data, or the settings they just changed, and taking the page away from them to say "please wait" would stop that. Every form that starts a long job carries class="js-long-run". The download form on the results page deliberately does not: it never navigates, so an indicator raised for it would never come down. #}
